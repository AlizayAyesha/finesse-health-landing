Finesse Health & Co. (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is an international dental concierge and patient coordination facilitator. We are not a healthcare provider, clinic, or hospital. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information — including sensitive health information — in connection with our administrative services, in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Finesse Health & Co. operates from Level 1, 93 George Street, Parramatta, NSW 2150, Australia. For privacy enquiries, access or correction requests, or complaints, contact us at finessehealthandco@gmail.com.au or +61 448 415 873.

2. What personal information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details: full name, phone number, email address, postal address, and preferred contact method.
  • Enquiry and travel details: preferred dental treatment type, travel preferences, passport-related information you choose to share for logistics coordination, and correspondence history.
  • Sensitive health information: dental charts, OPG or other radiographic images, clinical photographs, medical histories, medications, and related records that you voluntarily provide so we can transmit them to overseas treating clinics for assessment and quotation.
  • Technical data: limited website usage data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited (if analytics are enabled in future).

We only collect sensitive health information with your consent and for the purposes described in this policy and our Terms of Service & Medical Disclaimer.

3. How we collect information

We generally collect information directly from you when you:

  • submit an enquiry or assessment request via our website form or email;
  • upload or send dental records, X-rays, or photos;
  • speak with our coordinators by phone or messaging channels; or
  • engage us for travel and appointment coordination.

We may also receive information from overseas clinics (for example, treatment plan summaries or appointment confirmations) that relate to the facilitation services you have requested.

4. Why we collect and use your information (APP 6)

We use personal and health information to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide administrative, booking, and concierge facilitation;
  • securely transmit dental records to accredited third-party dental practitioners and clinical coordinators overseas (primarily in India) for assessment, triage, and quotation — with your explicit consent;
  • coordinate itineraries, transfers, and appointment schedules;
  • communicate with you about your enquiry and logistics;
  • comply with legal obligations and manage disputes relating to our facilitation services; and
  • improve our website and service quality (in aggregated or de-identified form where practicable).

We do not use your information to provide clinical diagnosis, treatment planning, or medical advice. Clinical decisions are made solely by independent treating clinics.

5. Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

By uploading or sending dental charts, OPG X-rays, medical histories, or clinical photographs, you explicitly consent to Finesse Health & Co. securely transmitting this sensitive health data to accredited third-party dental practitioners and clinical coordinators located overseas (primarily India) solely for assessment, triage, and quotation.

Overseas recipients may not be subject to the same privacy laws as Australia. We take reasonable steps to ensure overseas recipients handle your information consistently with this policy and the APPs, but you acknowledge that overseas disclosure carries residual risk. You may withdraw consent before transmission by contacting us; if records have already been sent to a clinic at your request, further control of those copies rests with that clinic under its own policies and local law.

6. Who we share information with

We may disclose your information to:

  • overseas treating dental clinics and their clinical coordinators (as above);
  • service providers who support our operations (for example, secure email, cloud storage, or future form processors), under appropriate confidentiality arrangements;
  • professional advisers (legal, accounting) where reasonably necessary; and
  • government agencies or courts where required or authorised by Australian law.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Storage and security (APP 11)

We maintain reasonable security measures to protect personal and health information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, modification, or disclosure. Measures may include access controls, encrypted transmission where available, and limiting staff access to need-to-know.

Important: The enquiry form on this website is currently a front-end demonstration. Until a secure backend is connected, do not rely on the web form for transmission of sensitive health records — please use the contact channels we confirm with you (for example, a designated secure email pathway).

8. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, to meet legal or accounting requirements, or to resolve disputes relating to our facilitation services. When information is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to destroy or de-identify it.

9. Access and correction (APPs 12 & 13)

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it if it is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant, or misleading. Contact us using the details above. We will respond within a reasonable period. In some cases we may refuse access where permitted by the Privacy Act; if so, we will explain why (unless we are legally prevented from doing so) and outline complaint options.

10. Cookies and website analytics

Our static site may use essential cookies required for basic functionality. If we introduce analytics or marketing cookies in future, we will update this policy and, where required, provide appropriate notice or consent mechanisms.

11. Complaints

If you believe we have breached your privacy, please contact us first so we can investigate and respond. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC): www.oaic.gov.au.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last Updated” date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of our website or coordination services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy to the extent permitted by law.

13. Related documents

Please also read our Terms of Service & Medical Disclaimer, which explain our non-clinical facilitator role, consent to overseas disclosure of health data, and limitation of liability.
