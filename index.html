How It Works

Four clear steps from enquiry to return

We coordinate the administrative pathway. Licensed specialists overseas deliver your clinical care.

  1. 01

    Submit Enquiry & Dental Records

    Share your details and securely upload dental records or OPG X-rays through our enquiry form. We review your submission for completeness before forwarding to a partner clinic.

  2. 02

    Custom Clinic Treatment Plan

    A licensed specialist clinic in India reviews your records and issues an individualized clinical quote and proposed treatment overview. We transmit that plan to you verbatim.

  3. 03

    Travel & Concierge Coordination

    Once you accept a plan, we help coordinate itinerary guidance, airport transfers, accommodation suggestions, and appointment schedules with the treating clinic.

  4. 04

    Treatment & Return

    You receive clinical care abroad from the treating dental clinic, with full administrative support from our coordinators throughout your stay and return journey.

Services Facilitated

Treatments we help you enquire about

We coordinate enquiries and logistics for common elective dental procedures performed by independent accredited clinics in India. We do not perform, diagnose, or warrant any clinical treatment.

  • Dental Implants

    We help you enquire about single, multiple, or full-arch implant options with partner specialist clinics.

  • Porcelain Veneers

    Coordinate assessments for aesthetic veneer treatments planned and delivered by overseas clinicians.

  • Full Mouth Rehabilitation

    Facilitate multi-phase rehabilitation quotes from clinics experienced in complex restorative cases.

  • Crowns & Bridges

    Enquire about crown and bridge restorations through accredited laboratory-supported clinics.

  • Smile Makeovers

    Help organise cosmetic smile planning consultations with treating dentists overseas.

  • Other Elective Procedures

    Ask us about additional elective dental treatments — we will confirm what partner clinics can assess.

About Us

Your dental travel concierge

Finesse Health & Co. is an independent international dental concierge and patient coordination service based in Parramatta, NSW. We exist to make premium dental care abroad more accessible for Australian patients — without pretending to be a clinic.

Our role is administrative: we help you share records securely, obtain clinic-issued treatment plans, coordinate travel logistics, and stay informed throughout the journey. Every diagnosis, surgery, and warranty sits exclusively with the treating dental clinic in India.

  • Independent booking & logistics facilitator
  • Secure transmission of dental records for overseas assessment
  • Concierge support before, during, and after travel
  1. 01

    Clarity first

    We explain our non-clinical role upfront and never claim to deliver treatment.

  2. 02

    Clinic-led plans

    Quotes and treatment overviews come from licensed overseas specialists, not our staff.

  3. 03

    Coordinated logistics

    Itineraries, transfers, and appointment schedules handled with care.
