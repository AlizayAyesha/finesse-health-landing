Services

General practice, done thoughtfully

A broad range of everyday medical care for individuals and families. Please call us to check availability for a specific service.

  • General consultations

    Appointments for new concerns, ongoing health questions, referrals and prescriptions.

  • Preventive health checks

    Routine check-ups and screening tailored to your age, history and lifestyle.

  • Chronic condition care

    Ongoing support and care planning for long-term health conditions.

  • Family & children’s health

    Care for all ages, from childhood check-ups through to healthy ageing.

  • Vaccinations

    Routine immunisations, including seasonal and travel vaccines where appropriate.

  • Women’s & men’s health

    Confidential, respectful advice on health matters specific to you.

Our approach

Healthcare with care and finesse

We believe good healthcare starts with a genuine conversation. Our clinic is designed to feel calm and unhurried, so you can talk openly about what matters to you.

Whether it’s a quick check-in or managing something more complex, we aim to explain your options clearly and work with you on a plan that fits your life.

  1. 01

    Time to listen

    Appointments focused on understanding you, not just your symptoms.

  2. 02

    Clear explanations

    Plain-language advice so you leave knowing your next steps.

  3. 03

    Coordinated care

    Help connecting with specialists and allied health when you need it.
